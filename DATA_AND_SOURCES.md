# Data, Sources and Evidence Policy

## Preferred official source organisations
Use primary source publications and preserve their exact definitions:
- Reserve Bank of India (RBI): https://www.rbi.org.in/
- National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB): https://ncrb.gov.in/
- Ministry of Home Affairs: https://www.mha.gov.in/
- Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C): https://i4c.mha.gov.in/
- National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in/
- CERT-In: https://www.cert-in.org.in/

## Required metadata for each statistic
Store and display:
- indicator name
- value and unit
- geography
- population/coverage
- period start and end
- publication date
- source organisation
- direct source URL
- definition/methodology
- last verified date
- status: official / secondary / illustrative

## Never conflate
- complaints received
- incidents reported
- FIRs / cases registered
- cases investigated or convicted
- bank fraud cases
- amount involved
- actual loss
- amount recovered

A bank fraud amount-involved figure is not automatically the same as money permanently lost. A cybercrime complaint count is not necessarily the same as a bank fraud case count.

## Demo-data policy
Use synthetic records for transaction simulations and dashboards when no authorised real dataset is available. Put “SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA — NOT LIVE BANK DATA” on the screen and in exported reports.
